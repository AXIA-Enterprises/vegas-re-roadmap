Monetization roadmap

An internal plan for turning 13 Las Vegas real-estate .com domains into a small number of real-estate advertising and lead-generation sites. Prepared October 2026.

Not legal advice. This document summarizes public statutes and regulator guidance for planning. Confirm every revenue model with a Nevada real-estate attorney and a licensed Nevada broker before you take money from anyone. See Legal & compliance.

Executive summary

Build three hub sites and point the rest at them. The keyword data supports only a few real search markets. Summerlin buying intent is the largest: "summerlin homes for sale" gets 1,200 searches a month at difficulty 3, and "summerlin real estate" gets 300. Next come valley-wide area searches such as "henderson homes for sale" (1,100) and "north las vegas homes for sale" (400). Then there is a small, high-value luxury and high-rise niche: "las vegas penthouse for sale" (200) and "las vegas high rise condos" (150), both at a $60 CPC. Seven of the 13 domains have no measurable search volume for their exact phrase (keyword table). Building 13 thin sites would also risk Google's doorway-abuse policy, which names "multiple domain names … targeted at specific regions or cities that funnel users to one page."

Hub 1 · summerlinlistings.com

Summerlin homes, condos and neighborhoods. Targets the largest low-difficulty keyword cluster (1,200 + 300 + 20 monthly searches).

Hub 2 · findavegasagent.com

An agent directory plus valley-wide area guides (Henderson, North Las Vegas, Centennial Hills, Rhodes Ranch). It is the natural place to sell featured-agent slots. "las vegas real estate agent" has a $450 CPC.

Hub 3 · lasvegashighriseliving.com

High-rises, penthouses and luxury condos across the valley, with a Summerlin section. Covers keywords worth $60 a click.

Sell advertising for a flat fee; don't take a cut of commissions. Nevada makes it unlawful for a licensee to pay "any part of a commission, compensation or finder's fee" to an unlicensed person (NRS 645.280). The definition of a real-estate broker covers anyone who, for compensation, "lists or solicits prospective purchasers" (NRS 645.030). Federal RESPA Section 8 bans fees for referring settlement business, and real-estate brokerage counts as a settlement service (12 U.S.C. 2607; 12 CFR 1024.2). That makes these the lower-risk models for an unlicensed owner:

  • flat monthly featured-agent and sponsorship placements
  • flat-fee featured listings supplied by the listing agent
  • clearly labelled sponsored articles

Per-closing referral fees, and very likely pay-per-lead pricing, need either a licensed Nevada broker to own that revenue line or a licensed entity controlled by the owner.

Live MLS listings (IDX) can only run under a broker. The Las Vegas REALTORS (formerly GLVAR) IDX participant form requires the signer to be "a principal real estate broker, broker in charge, or a real estate brokerage firm … licensed in accordance with the laws of the state of Nevada." The website must be under that broker's "direct supervision and control" (LVR/GLVAR IDX participant request). The path is a partner-broker agreement plus an approved IDX vendor. Until that is in place, the hubs run on editorial content and agent advertising.

Summit House is a proposed project, not a built one. As of 9 October 2026, Howard Hughes Holdings and Discovery Land Co. had filed plans for two 21-story towers with 260 units. Clark County had scheduled no hearings, and no pricing or construction timeline had been released (Hoodline, Oct 2026). The six Summit House domains therefore have no inventory to monetize yet, and they carry trademark and confusion risk against the developer. Park them for now, run a trademark check, and revisit them when the project's status changes.

Top recommendations

  1. Consolidate. Build three hubs, 301-redirect four zero- or no-data domains into them, and park the six Summit House domains (domain table).
  2. Get a legal opinion first. Ask a Nevada real-estate attorney to approve the flat-fee advertising agreement, the featured-agent terms and any lead product before you sell anything (Legal).
  3. Start with flat-fee ads. Launch with featured-agent slots and sponsorships at a fixed monthly price. The price must not depend on leads, referrals or closings. Rates are to be verified through test offers to local agents (Monetization).
  4. Sign a partner broker for IDX and referral revenue. Use an LVR-participant broker plus an approved IDX vendor, so per-closing economics run through a licensee (Lead gen & IDX).
  5. Write content for the keywords that exist. Order the content by search volume: "summerlin homes for sale", then "henderson homes for sale", then "north las vegas homes for sale", then "summerlin real estate". After those, cover the penthouse and high-rise pages (SEO plan).
