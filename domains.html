What the numbers say

  • Summerlin buyer intent is easy to target. "summerlin homes for sale" (1,200 searches) and "summerlin real estate" (300) both have a KD of 3. They are the best combination of volume and difficulty in the set.
  • Henderson has the biggest ceiling. "henderson homes for sale" has 1,100 searches but a traffic potential of 7,000, the highest in the set. That suggests a strong page ranks for many related queries. None of the domains contains "henderson", so it goes under the valley-wide hub.
  • "las vegas real estate agent" is the money keyword. Its $450 CPC is about seven times the next-highest CPC ($60), which signals how much agents pay to be found. It is also the only hard keyword in the set (KD 46). Treat it as a long-term target for findavegasagent.com.
  • The luxury and high-rise niche is small but expensive. The penthouse, high-rise condo and Henderson luxury keywords total 450 searches a month at a $60 CPC. That supports premium sponsorships more than traffic volume.
  • Most domain phrases have no searches. None of the exact-match phrases behind the redirect or park domains shows measurable search volume. Those domains are worth something as brandable names or redirect sources, not as standalone SEO sites.

Why the Summit House domains are parked

Summit House is a filed proposal, not an existing building. Hoodline reported on 9 October 2026 that Howard Hughes Holdings and Discovery Land Co. had filed plans for two 21-story towers with 260 condo units and a 17th-floor skybridge. Clark County had scheduled no hearings, and no pricing, cost estimate or construction timeline had been released (Hoodline; also covered by the Las Vegas Review-Journal).

Consequences:

  • There is nothing to list.
  • "summit house summerlin" has no search data.
  • Names like livesummithouse.com could be mistaken for the developer's own sales site.

Before any of these domains carries content or redirects, check whether the developer holds or has filed a "Summit House" trademark (to be verified with a USPTO search and an attorney). Holding them parked costs only renewals.

Redirect rules

  • Use server-side permanent redirects (301 or 308) from every URL on the old domain straight to the matching hub section. Don't chain redirects. Google says to "Use server side permanent redirects if technically possible." It also says to keep them "for as long as possible, generally at least 1 year" (Google Search Central).
  • Point each redirect at a relevant section, not the hub's homepage, so the old name matches what visitors land on.
  • Parked domains should show a minimal holding page or no content at all. Don't publish duplicate content across domains.
