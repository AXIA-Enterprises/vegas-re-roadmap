Phased roadmap, order of work & risks

Timeframes are planning estimates from the start date, not commitments. Each phase has a gate that must be met before the next one starts.

Phased roadmap

Phase 0: Foundations and legal review · weeks 0–4

  • Engage a Nevada real-estate attorney on the questions for counsel. Get written answers on flat-fee ads, subscriptions, the partner-broker structure, and the Summit House names.
  • Run a trademark search on "Summit House" (USPTO and Nevada) before any of the six domains carries content or redirects.
  • Form or confirm the operating entity. Draft standard advertising terms: flat fee; no dependence on leads or closings; licensee supplies license number and brokerage; broker approval of the ad.
  • Set up DNS and hosting for the three hubs. Point the six Summit House domains at a minimal holding page, or leave them unresolved.

Deliverables: attorney memo, ad-terms template, entity, hosting.
Gate: counsel approves at least the flat-fee advertising model.

Phase 1: Hub build and redirects · months 1–3

  • Launch summerlinlistings.com with content-plan items #1, #4 and #11 and the /villages/ pages.
  • Launch findavegasagent.com with #2, #3 and #6, a free basic agent directory, and the "how to check a license" guide.
  • Put the four 301 redirects in place: summerlinluxurylistings → /luxury/, summerlinhighriseliving and summerlintowers → lasvegashighriseliving.com/summerlin/, summerlinpenthouse → /penthouses/.
  • Set up Search Console and analytics on each hub. Create brand profiles and the Nevada directory citations (citations plan).
  • Collect local advertising rate quotes to replace the to be verified prices.

Deliverables: two live hubs (about 25 pages each), redirects verified, citation set.
Gate: pages indexed, with first impressions for the target keywords in Search Console.

Phase 2: First revenue from flat-fee advertising · months 3–6

  • Launch lasvegashighriseliving.com with #5, #7 and #12 and the building pages.
  • Offer featured-agent slots (2–3 per area page) and one presenting sponsorship per hub at a flat monthly price. Pitch 10–20 local agents and teams and record which prices they accept.
  • Open flat-fee featured listings, sold only to listing agents, and labelled sponsored articles.
  • Remaining area pages: #8, #9, #10.

Deliverables: media kit, order form with license and brokerage fields, first paying advertisers.
Gate: paying advertisers renew, and the sites receive steady organic traffic.

Phase 3: Partner broker, IDX and leads · months 6–12

  • Sign a partner agreement, reviewed by counsel, with an LVR-participant broker.
  • The broker files the IDX participant request for summerlinlistings.com through an approved vendor (IDX path), then for the other hubs.
  • Move lead pooling and any per-closing referral economics to the broker. The owner keeps a flat, fair-market fee.
  • Test territory subscriptions if counsel cleared them in Phase 0.

Deliverables: IDX live on Hub 1, broker agreement, lead-routing rules.
Gate: IDX compliance confirmed by the broker and MLS, and the revenue split confirmed by counsel.

Phase 4: Scale and portfolio decisions · months 12–18

  • Expand content around the keywords that converted to advertisers. Go after "las vegas real estate agent" (KD 46) with links earned from the market-data reports.
  • Decide what to do with the Summit House domains, based on the project's status and the trademark result: redirect one to lasvegashighriseliving.com/summerlin/, keep holding, or let them lapse.
  • Review the redirect domains' renewals. Keep them while redirects carry traffic or links; Google advises keeping redirects at least a year.

Deliverables: renewal and drop list, a year-2 content and sales plan.

Recommended order of work

  1. Get the Nevada real-estate attorney review: flat-fee ads, subscriptions, partner-broker structure, Summit House trademark.
  2. Park all six Summit House domains; make no content and no redirects yet.
  3. Set up hosting and DNS for summerlinlistings.com, findavegasagent.com and lasvegashighriseliving.com.
  4. Write and launch summerlinlistings.com/ for "summerlin homes for sale".
  5. Write and launch findavegasagent.com/henderson/ for "henderson homes for sale".
  6. Add the four 301 redirects into the hub sections.
  7. Publish findavegasagent.com/north-las-vegas/ and summerlinlistings.com/real-estate/.
  8. Finish the free agent directory and agent-ad order form (license number and brokerage required).
  9. Set up brand profiles and Nevada directory citations.
  10. Collect local rate quotes and set test prices.
  11. Launch lasvegashighriseliving.com (penthouses, high-rise index, building pages, Summerlin tracker).
  12. Sell the first featured-agent slots and sponsorships.
  13. Recruit a partner broker and file the IDX request.
  14. Route leads and referral economics through the broker.
  15. Review results and make the Summit House and renewal decisions.
